Q1: Why might we consider using Git?

Answer: We should consider using Git because it makes version control much easier, and when used with a platform like GitHub, it makes collaboration much easier. Using Git, doing things like reverting to a known good version or saving changes becomes much easier because you have a history of all your commits.

Q2: What field could data science be applied to? Give an Example.

Answer: Data science could be applied to finance, for example detecting credit card fraud.

Q3: What is the difference between Merging and Rebasing?

Answer: Merging combines branches by creating a new commit that preserves the complete history, but rebasing rewrites history by moving your branch's commits onto the tip of the target branch for a linear log/commit history.
